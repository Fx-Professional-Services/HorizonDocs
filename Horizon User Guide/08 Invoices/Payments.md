
# Payments

Payments are used to record and track payments made against sales orders. This feature helps you manage the financial transactions related to your sales orders.

#### To access and manage payments:

1. In the main menu, click **Invoice**.
    
2. Click **Payments**.

##### Delete Payments

3. In the left pane, select the payment record you want to delete.
4. Click **Delete Record**.
5.  In the confirmation dialog, click **Delete** to proceed.
