# Payment Terms

The **Payment Terms** section in Horizon Accounting allows you to define and manage the terms under which invoices are paid. Setting payment terms ensures that your invoices are issued with clear expectations regarding payment deadlines and conditions. This feature is essential for streamlining your billing process and maintaining consistent cash flow.

##### Access **Payment Terms**:

1. From the main menu, select **Invoices** to open Horizon Accounting.
2. In the secondary menu bar, click **Options**, then choose **Payment Terms**.

##### Delete Payment Terms

3. In the left pane, select the payment term you want to delete.
4. Click **Delete Record**.
5.  In the confirmation dialog, click **Delete** to proceed.
