# Receiving Order

A **Receiving Order** is the process of receiving items from a **Purchase Order** and other additional items, ensuring that stock levels are updated to reflect newly-arrived inventory. Follow these steps to create a receiving order:

1. **Navigate to the Main Menu**

- From the main menu, select **Sales Orders**.

2. **Access Order Management**

- Click **Receiving Orders**.

3. **Create a New Record**

- Click **New Record**.

4. **Select a Party**

- Click the **Party Display Name** box.
- Choose the party’s name from the picker. The party should have an existing confirmed **Purchase Order** to pick the purchase order item ID.
- Click the add button (✚), then click **Done** at the bottom.

5. Specify Item Type

- In the **Receiving Order Items** section, choose either **Purchase Order Item** or **Item**.

6. Select Items

- Click the box labeled **Click to select in the picker**.
- In the picker, select the items you want to add.

7. Confirm the Order

- Click the **Confirm** button to confirm the receiving order.
    - This will change the order status from **Pending** to **Confirmed**.
    - It will also update the inventory.

**To Verify the Inventory Update:**

- Go to **Item Management** > **All Items**.
- Switch to **Find Mode** and enter the item name in the **Name** field.
- The **Received** field should display the updated value.

8. **Unconfirm the Order**

- To unconfirm the order, click **Unconfirm**.
    - This will change the order status from **Confirmed** to **Pending** and decrease the inventory.

**To Verify the Inventory Update:**

- Follow the same steps as outlined in the **Verify the Inventory Update** section.

9. **Save or Undo Changes**

- Click **Save Changes** to keep your modifications.
- Click **Undo Changes** to discard them.

10. **Delete an Order**

- To delete a specific order, select **Delete Record**.
    - ⚠️ **Note**: You can only delete a receiving order if it has been confirmed. To confirm the order, click **Confirm**. Confirm the deletion by clicking **Yes** in the dialogue box.
