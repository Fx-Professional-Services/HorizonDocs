# Move Orders

A **move order** directs the relocation of a specific quantity of items from one location to another within a warehouse. It specifies both the source and destination locations, ensuring items are positioned correctly to fulfill a [pick order](#pick-order) efficiently. 

Follow these steps to create a move order:

1. From the main menu, go to **Sales Orders**. 

2. Click **Order Management,** then select **Move Order**. 

<img src="https://github.com/Fx-Professional-Services/HorizonDocs/blob/staging/Horizon%20User%20Guide/00%20Assets/72_all_items.png" width="350" height="250">

3. Click **New Record**. <br>
	a. Select an employee. <br>
	b. Add **Move Order Items**. <br>
<img src="https://github.com/Fx-Professional-Services/HorizonDocs/blob/staging/Horizon%20User%20Guide/00%20Assets/73_move_orders.png" width="350" height="250">

c. Choose the current and new locations. <br>
	⚠️ Note: If the location name is unavailable, only the location serial will be displayed. <br>
d. Enter the quantity of items you want to move. <br>
<img src="https://github.com/Fx-Professional-Services/HorizonDocs/blob/staging/Horizon%20User%20Guide/00%20Assets/74_add_move_order_items.png" width="350" height="250">


4.  Verify the location transfer by going to **Item Management** > **Locations**. Ensure that the quantity of the product in both the "From" and "To" locations has been updated according to your selection.

<img src="https://github.com/Fx-Professional-Services/HorizonDocs/blob/staging/Horizon%20User%20Guide/00%20Assets/75_verify_location.png" width="350" height="250">
