# My Tasks

The **My Tasks** section is an essential tool for managing your responsibilities within a project. It provides a centralized view of all tasks assigned to you, allowing you to easily track progress, prioritize work, and ensure deadlines are met. Use this feature to stay organized and efficiently manage your contributions to the project.

## Create a New Project Record

1. From the main menu, click **My Tasks**.
2. Select **Projects** from the primary navigation tabs.
3. Click **New Record** to create a new task record.
4. In the **Details** tab, enter the **Project Name**.
5. Click the **Party Display Name** field to select a party from the picker.
6. Choose the **Due Date** by selecting a date from the pop-up calendar.
7. In the **Parties** section, the party selected in the **Party Display Name** field is automatically added. To add another party, click **Click to add party** and select a party from the picker.
8. Assign a role to each party (e.g., **Assignee**, **Manager**, or **Watcher**). 

💡 **Tip**: To remove a party, click the **Delete button** (x) next to the party name. To remove all parties, click **Delete All**.

9. In the **Items (Assets)** section, click **Click too add item** to add an item required for the task. 

10. In the **Tasks** section, enter the task name to create a task.

💡 **Tip**: To remove a task, click the **Delete button** (x) next to the task name. To remove all tasks, click **Delete All**.

11. Next to the **Task Name** field, enter the due date for the task.
12. Once all details are entered, click **Save Changes**.
