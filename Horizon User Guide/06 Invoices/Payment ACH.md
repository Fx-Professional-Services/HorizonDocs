
Test Guide


In the Main Menu, click on Invoices. This will take you to the Invoice module in Horizon Accounting. 

Click on Payments, then click **Options** to go to **Payment ACH** module.

Click on the **New Record** button.

Click on **Party Display Name** to select a customer.

Enter ACH details: account type (checking or savings), account number, routing number, bank name, memo (optional) and last 4 digits.
